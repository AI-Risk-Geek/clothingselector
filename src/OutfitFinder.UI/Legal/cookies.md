## The cookies we use
One cookie (`gf_visitor`) remembers your looks on this device, and only after you accept it. When you sign in, one more cookie keeps you signed in. A tiny setting in your browser remembers light or dark mode. That is all.

## What we do not use
No advertising cookies and no third-party tracking.

## Your choice
If you decline, the site still works but your looks are not remembered between visits. You can change your mind at any time from the footer link "Cookie settings".
