## What we collect
The photos you upload, the clothing results we find, your username, your email address if you choose to give one, and body measurements if you choose to add them. If you accept our cookie, we also keep a random visitor ID so your looks are remembered on this device.

## How photos are handled
Each photo is first checked for unsafe content by {CONTENT_SAFETY_PROVIDER}. It is then sent to our AI provider ({AI_PROVIDER}) to find the clothing and where to buy it. Faces are blurred, and only the blurred copy is saved. The original upload is deleted as soon as the search finishes. Photos that fail the safety check are never stored; we keep only a fingerprint (hash) and the reason, to stop repeat uploads.

## How long we keep it
Photos and results are deleted after 90 days. You can delete any search, or everything, at any time from Account & privacy.

## Who processes your data
{HOSTING_PROVIDER} hosts the service. {AI_PROVIDER} analyses photos. {CONTENT_SAFETY_PROVIDER} checks photos for unsafe content. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

## Your rights
You can download your data, correct it, or delete your account and everything in it from Account & privacy. You can also contact us at {PRIVACY_EMAIL}. If you are in the EU or UK you may complain to your data protection authority.

## Children
{BRAND} is for people aged 16 and over. Photos that appear to show children are refused and not stored.
