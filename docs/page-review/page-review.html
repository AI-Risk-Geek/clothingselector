Update, 26 November: high and medium issues fixed

The 6 high and 7 medium issues are fixed and checked again at phone width. These are the new screenshots, taken after the fixes. The original findings below are kept for reference.

High priority

High

Privacy Policy shows unfilled placeholders

/legal/privacy

Visitors can read "[AI PROVIDER]", "[CONTENT SAFETY PROVIDER]", "[HOSTING PROVIDER]" and "[PRIVACY EMAIL]". Fill these from settings the same way the brand name is filled, and refuse to start in Production while any placeholder remains.

High

"Make it" is a dead end for most pieces

Results › Make it

The first piece selected is the sunglasses, so the first tap on "Make it" says "No pattern for this piece yet". Only show "Make it" on sewable pieces, or put a scissors mark on the chips that have a pattern and open the first sewable piece by default.

High

The extension page contradicts itself and doesn't help phone visitors

/install

The button says "Chrome Web Store listing coming soon", but step 1 says "One click from the Chrome Web Store". The disabled button also wraps onto two lines and looks broken. Phone visitors (who can't use the extension) get no next step: add "Email me the link" or "Remind me on my computer". The permissions table squeezes its first column to one word per line on a phone; stack the rows instead.

High

Reset password page works without a reset link

sign-in server /Account/ResetPassword

Opened without a code, it still shows the new-password form, which can only fail. Show "This link is missing or has expired" with a button to request a new one.

High

An unknown legal page is a blank "Page not found"

/legal/anything-else

It has a heading and nothing else. The real 404 page explains what happened and offers "Find a look"; reuse it here.

High

AI settings shows no provider selected

/admin/ai

When the provider comes from app configuration (here Demo), none of the three provider cards is highlighted, so it looks unset. Show the active provider as a selected, read-only card, and say that saving a provider here replaces the one from configuration.

Medium

Medium

The cookie banner covers the upload buttons on a first phone visit

Home

It hides "Take a photo" and the human check. "Accept" looks like plain text beside a white "Decline" button. Make the two buttons look the same, and leave space below the page so the banner never covers content.

Medium

The loading screen shows an empty grey box

Finding your look

Show the person's own photo under the scan line. It is the strongest sign that something is happening.

Medium

Fix the boxes is hard to use with a finger

Results › Fix the boxes

The resize handles are 22 px (aim for 44). On the small sunglasses box the handle covers the number, and the trousers and shoes boxes overlap so their handles collide. "Cancel" sits alone on a third row.

Medium

Looks in history are hard to tell apart

/looks

Two looks of the same photo show the same picture, "5 pieces" and "Today". Name each look by its main piece ("Linen camp shirt + 4") and show the time.

Medium

Report content form

/legal/takedown

The reason text is cut off ("…without permissio"). When it is opened from a look, fill in the link to that look automatically. Validation messages are clear.

Medium

Account page details

/account

The measurement boxes don't show "cm" and there's no "how to measure" help. "Cookie settings" tells people to find a footer link instead of giving them a button. "Clear history" and "Delete account" wrap onto two lines on a phone.

Medium

Results actions wrap, with Delete left on its own

Results

Delete, a destructive action, ends up centred and prominent on a second row. Keep "Fix the boxes" and "Search again", and move Delete into a "⋯" menu.

Low

Low

Smaller polish

  • The sign-in pages follow the phone's dark setting but not the site's own dark-mode toggle (screenshot). The demo banner is also missing there.
  • The signed-out page has two "back" links, one in the header and one as a button (screenshot).
  • On a phone the legal tabs wrap, leaving "Report content" alone on a second row (screenshot).
  • On desktop, a single look highlights "My looks" in the menu. That's fine if intended, but anonymous visitors may not think of it as theirs (screenshot).

What works well

Good
  • No page scrolls sideways at 390 px, and no page logged a console error.
  • Dark mode is consistent across the main site (results, home, account).
  • Form errors are specific and next to the field (sign in, report content).
  • Signing in moved the looks found on this device into the account: two looks appeared in history.
  • The 404 and error pages are friendly and offer a way back (404, error).
  • Admin pages redirect to sign-in when signed out.
